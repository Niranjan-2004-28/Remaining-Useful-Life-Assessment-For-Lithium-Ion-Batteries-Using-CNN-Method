About The Project

Our Lithium Battery RUL Prediction System leverages cutting-edge Convolutional Neural Networks to accurately estimate the remaining useful life of lithium-ion batteries.

With the increasing demand for reliable energy storage systems, predicting battery health and lifespan has become crucial for industries ranging from electric vehicles to renewable energy storage.

Traditional methods often fail to capture complex degradation patterns, but our deep learning approach provides superior accuracy by analyzing battery performance data and identifying subtle patterns that precede failure.

Key Features

Deep Learning Model

Utilizes advanced CNN architecture trained on extensive battery degradation data for superior prediction accuracy.

Real-time Monitoring

Continuously analyzes battery performance data to provide up-to-date RUL estimates and health status.

Early Warning System

Detects early signs of battery degradation and provides alerts before critical failure occurs.

How It Works

1 Data Collection

The system collects battery performance data including voltage, current, temperature, and charge/discharge cycles.

2 Feature Extraction

Key features are extracted from the raw data to capture degradation patterns and health indicators.

3 CNN Processing

Our trained convolutional neural network analyzes the features to identify complex degradation patterns.

4 RUL Prediction

The system outputs the estimated remaining useful life along with confidence metrics.
